Stuart answers questions about Mezo and Matchbox in Pro. Open it from the Ctrl K bar, and every number in an answer carries its source and block.

## What Stuart answers {#what-stuart-answers}

- Your locks, votes, rewards and loan, read from the chain and Matchbox.
- Which gauges pay the most, and why one is in or out of your best split.
- How Mezo Earn works, from reviewed notes with their sources.

## Where the numbers come from {#numbers}

Matchbox services work out every figure, never the language model. A sentence with a number appears only after that number matched the tools' results for the question.

## Prepared transactions {#prepared-transactions}

Ask Stuart to vote, claim or change a lock, and it prepares the steps for you to review. Your wallet or your Safe signs them, and Stuart never says a transaction was sent.

## Limits {#limits}

Stuart answers in Pro, and these docs link there. Busy answers say so and suggest when to try again.
