Matchbox ID is the account behind Sign in with Matchbox. You sign in with your wallet, and apps you allow get a stable id and only what you agree to share.

:::facts

- **Address** `id.matchbox.markets`
- **Sign in with** Your wallet, no fee
- **Linked account** Discord, optional
- **For developers** [Sign in with Matchbox](/developer/oidc)

:::

## Your account {#your-account}

| Tab            | What it shows                                |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Connected apps | Apps you allowed, what they can read, Revoke |
| Sessions       | Where you are signed in, Sign out            |
| Appearance     | Mode and theme presets                       |
