---
title: Appearance
description: Mode and theme presets in Matchbox ID.
updated: 2026-11-10
---

The Appearance tab sets the mode and the theme presets for Matchbox ID. It is the same picker as in Pro.

Pick Light, Dark or System, then a light and a dark preset. The choice is saved on this device, and the icon beside the title says so.

:::note[Not synced yet]
Your choice is shared with the other Matchbox products in this browser. It does not sync to your account yet, so another device keeps its own.
:::
