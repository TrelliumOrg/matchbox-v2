title: Sessions
description: Where you are signed in to Matchbox ID, and how to sign out of each.
updated: 2026-11-10
---

Sessions lists every device signed in to your Matchbox ID. The one you are using is marked This device.

Each row shows the device, part of its IP address and when it was last seen. Press Sign out on a row to end that session.

## Sign out others {#sign-out-others}

Sign out others ends every session but this one. Use it if you signed in on a device you no longer have.

A Matchbox ID session lasts 7 days.
