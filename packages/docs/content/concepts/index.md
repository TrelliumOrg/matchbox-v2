Mezo Earn pays people who lock tokens and vote. These pages explain the pieces once, and every Matchbox product links here.

## The short version {#the-short-version}

Lock BTC as veBTC or MEZO as veMEZO, and the lock gets voting power. Each week you point that power at gauges, and the gauges you vote for pay you their incentives.

| Piece      | What it is                                         |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Epoch      | One week, Thursday 00:00 UTC to the next Thursday  |
| veBTC      | BTC locked for up to 28 days, votes on two voters  |
| veMEZO     | MEZO locked for up to 208 weeks, votes on two more |
| Gauge      | Something you vote for, with incentives on it      |
| Boost      | Extra voting power veMEZO gives a veBTC lock       |
| Incentives | Tokens anyone adds to a gauge for its voters       |
