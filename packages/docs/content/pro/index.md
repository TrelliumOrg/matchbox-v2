Matchbox Pro is where you lock, vote and claim on Mezo Earn. It shows every gauge you can vote on, projects what each split would earn, and signs one step at a time.

:::facts

- **Address** `pro.matchbox.markets`
- **Network** Mezo
- **Pages** Overview, Vote, Rewards, Activity, Protocol
- **Signs with** Your wallet, or a Safe

:::

## How it works {#how-it-works}

::::steps

:::step[Lock]
Lock BTC as veBTC or MEZO as veMEZO. The lock's voting power is what you vote with.
:::

:::step[Vote]
Split each lock's votes across gauges of the types it can vote on. Votes stay in place from epoch to epoch.
:::

:::step[Claim]
When an epoch ends, claim what your votes earned. Claim and relock puts the MEZO back into a veMEZO lock.
:::

::::
