Overview is Pro's home page. Before a wallet connects it shows Mezo Earn as a whole, and after it shows your locks and what they earn.

## Before connecting {#before-connecting}

- Incentives this epoch, and how many gauges are funded.
- How much veMEZO voted, out of all veMEZO locked.
- Top gauges, with Browse gauges to open Vote.
- How it works: Lock, Vote, Claim.

## With a wallet {#with-a-wallet}

- Claimable now, and Projected for the current epoch.
- Claim all, Claim and relock, and Vote.
- Your lock cards, each with its epoch figures.
- A suggestion when a veBTC gauge is short of 5×, with Add votes.
- Recent activity.

A wallet with no locks sees what it has available to lock, with a button to lock it.

## Lock cards {#lock-cards}

Each lock card shows the amount and its voting power. Press Manage to flip it to its actions, as [Locks](/pro/locks) explains.

The rows read Not voted in an epoch the lock has no votes, and Changes saved for the next epoch when a split is waiting.
