Activity lists what a wallet did, in plain words. Protocol shows Mezo Earn as a whole, one epoch at a time.

## Activity {#activity}

Activity groups a wallet's votes, claims, lock actions and incentives by day. Filter it with All, Votes, Claims, Locks and Incentives, or search by lock, gauge or transaction.

Open a row for its details and status: Submitted, Confirming, Reverted or Confirmed.

## Protocol {#protocol}

| Part                  | What it shows                                  |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Incentives this epoch | The total, against the epoch before            |
| Locked                | veMEZO and veBTC locked, and veBTC vote weight |
| Median APY            | Across funded gauges                           |
| Incentives by epoch   | A chart, by gauge type                         |
| Funded this epoch     | The gauges with incentives, with See all       |
| Live activity         | Recent transactions, with Export CSV           |

Step through epochs with the epoch switcher, or open one by its number at `/protocol/47`.
