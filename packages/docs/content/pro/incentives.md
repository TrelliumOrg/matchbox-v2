Add incentives to a gauge to draw votes to it. They pay that gauge's voters for the current epoch.

::::steps

:::step[Open the gauge]
Open the gauge's page from Vote or Overview, then press Add incentives.
:::

:::step[Pick a token and amount]
Choose MUSD, MEZO or BTC and enter the amount. Pro checks that the gauge accepts the token.
:::

:::step[Review and sign]
The review shows the epoch it pays, the day it pays and Withdrawable: No. The toast reads Incentives added.
:::

::::

:::warning[No take backs]
Incentives cannot be withdrawn once added. They always go to the current epoch, so add them early in the week.
:::

## How they pay {#how-they-pay}

The gauge's voters split the incentives by voting weight when the epoch ends. See [Incentives](/concepts/incentives) for the rules and the tokens each voter allows.

## Gauge profiles {#gauge-profiles}

Pro shows each gauge's profile on its page: About, Strategy and Owner. Editing a profile is not in Pro yet.
