Short answers, each with the page that explains more.

## Do I need to vote every week? {#vote-every-week}

Votes stay in place from epoch to epoch until you vote again or the lock is reset. See [Epochs](/concepts/epochs).

## Can I change my vote this epoch? {#change-vote}

Not on chain once the lock has voted this epoch. Pro saves your new split and offers it when the next epoch opens, as [Vote](/pro/vote#voting-again-this-epoch) explains.

## When can I claim? {#when-claim}

When the epoch ends, Thursday 00:00 UTC. See [Rewards](/pro/rewards).

## How long can I lock? {#how-long}

veBTC up to 28 days and veMEZO up to 208 weeks. A lock only has to reach the next Thursday 00:00 UTC.

## Can I unlock early? {#unlock-early}

No. A lock is withdrawn after its unlock date, and Auto max must be turned off first.

## Why did my boost drop? {#boost-drop}

Boost compares shares of all veBTC and all veMEZO, so it falls when others lock or vote more. See [Boost](/concepts/boost).

## Which tokens can I add as incentives? {#incentive-tokens}

Pro offers MUSD, MEZO and BTC, from the tokens each voter allows. See [Incentives](/concepts/incentives).

## Does Pro hold my funds? {#custody}

No. Every step is signed by your wallet or your Safe, and Pro never asks for an approval of your lock.
