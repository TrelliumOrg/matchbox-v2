Matchscan shows Mezo Earn from the outside: every gauge, every epoch and every lock. Voting itself happens in Pro.

## Gauges {#gauges}

Gauges lists every gauge, by type: veBTC gauges, MEZO gauges, Validators and Pools. Sort by votes, rewards, APY or voters.

A gauge page shows its votes and incentives per epoch, who votes on it and the incentive deposits. Pool gauges add the pool's volume and liquidity, and killed gauges are marked Inactive.

## Epochs {#epochs}

Epochs lists every epoch with its dates, emissions, votes cast, voters and incentives. The current epoch is marked Current.

An epoch page breaks those figures down per gauge type. Read [Epochs](/concepts/epochs) for the clock itself.

## Locks {#locks}

Locks lists veBTC and veMEZO locks. Order them by largest, unlocking soon or newest.

A lock page shows its votes by epoch and its history. Manage on Matchbox opens it in Pro.
