Borrow in Matchscan shows every loan on Mezo. Borrowing itself happens on Mezo, and positions link there with Borrow on Mezo or Manage on Mezo.

## Tabs {#tabs}

| Tab          | What it shows                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overview     | Markets, the loans closest to liquidation, recent liquidations      |
| Markets      | Morpho Blue markets with LLTV, borrow APR, utilization and borrowed |
| Positions    | Morpho Blue positions                                               |
| Liquidations | Trove liquidations                                                  |
| Troves       | Open MUSD troves, the Stability Pool and redemptions                |

## Pages {#pages}

- A market shows what is supplied and borrowed over time, its positions and its settings.
- A Morpho position shows its health, collateral, debt and the BTC price it liquidates under.
- A trove shows its collateral ratio, collateral, debt and interest.
- An Enclave position shows its rate, minimum ratio and the veBTC pledged.

Health reads red near liquidation, amber as it approaches and green when healthy. See [Borrow](/concepts/borrow) for the thresholds.
