# Pro motion audit

Every moving part of Pro, what it does, and what it did before the motion
pass of 9 Nov 2026 (main at 6c070ed). Ledger items M1 to M7 and P2.

Rules (matchbox-v3, Motion with life; `agent-instructions/performance-and-motion.md`):

- Transform, opacity and stroke-dashoffset only.
- 120 to 260ms for small things, at most 400ms for sheets, 600ms for count-ups
  and chart draws. Curves: `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)` (roll) for slides and
  rolls, `cubic-bezier(.19,1,.22,1)` for text.
- Exits mirror entries and run before unmount. Whatever is leaving takes no
  clicks, a new navigation or open cancels it, nothing waits on it.
- The server renders final values. Count-ups never run on the LCP element.
  CLS stays 0.
- Reduced motion: final values, a short fade or nothing (`[data-mbx-motion]`
  in `@matchbox/ui/styles.css`, `motionStep` in `@matchbox/motion`).

Shared presets live in `@matchbox/motion` (`presets.ts`: sheet, drawer,
bottomSheet, scrim, popover, toast, list, indicator; `indicator.ts`;
`presence.ts`; `presence-list.ts`; `count-up.ts`; `flip-run.ts`), tested in
`presets.test.tsx`.

## Haptics (phones, gated by the Haptics setting)

Pick `[8]` on gauge pick and unpick and Stuart choices, signed `[10, 70, 14]`
on a confirmed step, failed `[12, 45, 12, 45, 12]` on a rejected or reverted
step, Optimize `[14, 55, 8]` when Optimize changes the ballot. Never on load,
scroll or timers.

## Reduced motion

Sheets, drawers, popovers and tooltips appear and go at once; lists, tabs,
indicators and charts jump to their final state; count-ups do not run;
digits and words change in place; press scale is off. `scripts/motion-record.mjs`
checks that nothing moves for more than 160ms under reduced motion.

## Verifying

Against a running preview (`pnpm preview`):

```sh
node scripts/motion-record.mjs <outDir>
```

It samples each moving element every frame through each transition (sheets
open and close, the Account panel and its sub-views, Stuart, popovers,
count-ups, chart draw and morph, segmented pill, type indicator, grid
reorder and filter, gauge pick, sidebar pill) at 1440 and 390 in light and
dark, and 1440 with reduced motion. It writes `report.json`, a video per run
and, with ffmpeg on the PATH, frames at 20, 50 and 80% of each transition.
It exits non-zero when something that should move does not, or something
moves under reduced motion. `e2e/motion.spec.ts` keeps the exit rules in CI.
